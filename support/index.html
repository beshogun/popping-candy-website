How do I play?

  1. Tap three empty spaces to place your poppers.
  2. Tap a placed popper to choose which one starts the chain. Undo lets you change your arrangement before firing.
  3. Press POP. A blast can trigger other poppers and fizzy crystals. Obstacles stop it.
  4. Clear the sweet goal to move forward. Replay a board to try a better route.

Open Settings → How to play to revisit the introduction. There is no countdown while you plan.

How does scoring work?

Each cleared sweet scores points. The wave multiplier grows from ×1 to ×2, ×4, ×8 and onward, up to ×64. Connected explosions add a chain bonus. “This round” and “Sweet goal” show the current attempt and reset when a new board starts; total points and personal records remain saved.

Where are my scores and progress?

The trophy button opens your records. Progress is stored on this device, with no Popping Candy account or cloud-save service. Deleting the app or resetting progress can remove local saves. Game Center rankings, when available, use your Apple Game Center account and are separate from your local progress.

How do ads and hints work?

Ads may appear between completed rounds, with a gap between them. An optional rewarded ad can provide a hint when available. Ads need an internet connection and may be unavailable; ordinary gameplay remains available. Test adverts do not generate revenue. There are no active in-app purchases or subscriptions in the first release.

Can I change privacy choices?

In Settings, choose “Usage analytics” to allow or turn off gameplay analytics. “Ad privacy choices” appears when Google's consent service requires it for your region. Turning gameplay analytics off does not change your separate ad privacy choices. Read the privacy policy for details.

Something is wrong

Email your app version (shown in Settings), device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot is helpful. Never include passwords, payment information or Apple verification codes. You can optionally export a playtest report from Settings and inspect it before sharing.

If a score stays at zero throughout an explosion, include the level number and whether you are using a TestFlight build. If an advert fails to load, you should still be able to continue playing.
